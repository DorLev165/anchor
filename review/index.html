How a session is routed

Situation→0–10→Recognize→Identify→Route→One small step→Follow-up→Learn

Anxiety of 8 or more inserts breathing first and trims the middle to a single tool. Otherwise breathing is never forced — it is offered once, on the Recognize screen.

What to look for

  • Reassurance — anything implying the feared thing won’t happen, or that it will be fine.
  • A step that doesn’t fit the situation, or isn’t doable right now.
  • Anything that rewards avoidance, checking, or reassurance-seeking.
  • Any implication that you must be calm before you can act.
  • In Check the story — anything that declares the belief untrue, rather than holding it up against the facts.

  2. Recognize — Health only

    Your anxiety is up right now.

    That can affect what you feel in your body — and what your mind is telling you. Anxiety can make sensations louder and harder to read. That doesn’t settle what any sensation means — it means anxiety is part of what you’re feeling right now.

    Never says “it’s just anxiety” and never rules out a medical cause.

  3. Check the story — where it is offered

    Never automatic.

    Offered in three places, always as a secondary option: after the loop card (“This one’s about me — check the story”), after Look at it straight when the named fear reads as a belief about yourself, and under “I’m not sure” as “It’s more about how I see myself”. Choosing it goes straight to one small step afterwards — no further analysis.

    Checked the same belief before with nothing new since? It stops and says: “No new information — not a new problem to solve.”

  4. Decision guard

    That’s a decision — not yet an action.

    Shown when the chosen step is a decision (“whether I reach out or let it rest”). Asks “What feels right for now?”, then turns the answer into an action — “Send one honest message.” / “Leave it for now and go back to what you were doing.”
